The 4AT

  • CurrentFree to use under the CC BY 4.0 licence, with no registration. More than 30 diagnostic accuracy studies. More than 20 languages.the4AT.com
  • 2023NICE guideline CG103 recommends the 4AT for assessment when indicators of delirium are found in hospital or long-term care, except in critical care or the recovery room after surgeryNICE
  • 2019SIGN 157 recommends the 4AT to identify probable delirium in emergency departments and acute hospitalsSIGN
  • 2021HSE Ireland guidance: screen people aged 65 or over with the 4AT at triage in the emergency departmentHSE guidance
  • 2025NHS England best practice tariff for hip fracture (2025/26): a 4AT before surgery and a repeat 4AT during the admissionNHS England
  • 2025Scotland: 16,476 of 18,040 people with hip fracture (91%) had a 4AT, in a study of national audit dataPubMed
