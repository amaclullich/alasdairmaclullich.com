Alasdair MacLullich

I am Professor of Geriatric Medicine at the University of Edinburgh and work in acute geriatric medicine and acute orthogeriatrics (care of older people with fractures) at the Royal Infirmary of Edinburgh.

I work on recognising delirium, assessing it well and helping clinicians and families know what to do next.

Current roles

  • Professor of Geriatric Medicine, University of EdinburghUniversity profile
  • Responsible for updates to the 4AT delirium assessment tool, which I designed in 2011 with Tracy Ryan and Helen Cash. NICE and SIGN recommend it.the4AT.com
  • Editor-in-Chief, Delirium, the journal of the European Delirium AssociationEditorial board
  • Chair, Scottish Hip Fracture AuditPublic Health Scotland

Books

    Published 31 July 2026. Delirium Support Press. 102 pages.
