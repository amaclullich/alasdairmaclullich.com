Alasdair MacLullich: Clinical work, research and public resources

I am Professor of Geriatric Medicine at the University of Edinburgh and work in acute geriatric medicine and acute orthogeriatrics (care of older people with fractures) at the Royal Infirmary of Edinburgh.

My work aims to improve how delirium is recognised, assessed, explained and acted on, from clinical care and the 4AT to practical information for families. This site brings the work together and directs each reader to the right resource.

Published 31 July 2026

The 4AT Manual: A Practical Guide to Delirium Assessment

The manual explains how to administer, score and interpret the 4AT, with thirty worked clinical cases and guidance on implementation and audit. It is written for nurses, doctors, allied health professionals, students and educators.

The full manual is free to read or download as a PDF or EPUB, with no registration.
