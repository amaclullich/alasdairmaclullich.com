Privacy on this website

This is a static website with no forms, accounts, comments or newsletters. Limited Google Analytics is on unless you turn it off.

Who is responsible

I, Alasdair MacLullich, operate this independent professional website and am responsible for its limited use of Google Analytics. For questions about this notice, please email a.maclullich@gmail.com or use my University of Edinburgh profile.

This notice covers alasdairmaclullich.com. The organisations and services linked from this website have their own privacy notices.

Information collected

Information you submit

There is no form on this website, so it does not directly collect names, email addresses, messages or other information from visitors.

Technical records

The site is hosted using GitHub Pages. As with most web hosting, GitHub may process technical information such as IP addresses and browser details for delivery, security and operational purposes. GitHub describes its handling of this information in the GitHub General Privacy Statement.

Cookies and analytics

Limited Google Analytics (measurement ID G-DEJ9C50DTY) loads when a page opens unless you have turned it off. It is used only to produce statistical information about how the website is used and to improve it. It records standard page views, including the page address and title, so that I can understand which pages are visited. Page locations keep campaign tags (utm_ parameters) and drop any other query string. Enhanced measurement, granular location and device collection, Google Signals, advertising and profiling are disabled.

The notice shown on your first visit explains this and lets you keep analytics on or turn it off. Your choice is recorded in essential storage. You can use the whole website either way and change your choice through Analytics settings in the footer.

am_analytics_choice
A first-party preference cookie that remembers whether you kept analytics on or turned it off. It lasts for 90 days.
_ga
A limited Google Analytics cookie used to distinguish visits. It lasts for no more than 90 days.
_ga_DEJ9C50DTY
A limited Google Analytics cookie used to maintain session information. It lasts for no more than 90 days.

Basis, retention and choices

For storage and access on your device, this website relies on the statistical purposes exception in the Privacy and Electronic Communications Regulations. For any related personal data processing, the lawful basis is my legitimate interest in understanding and improving this website. You can object free of charge at any time through Analytics settings in the footer. Turning analytics off stops future loading and removes the _ga cookies that this site can access. The preference remains for 90 days so that the site remembers your choice.

Google Analytics retains event and user data for 14 months. Returning to the site does not extend this period. Google Signals and advertising personalisation features are disabled. Google may process analytics data outside the United Kingdom and describes the safeguards it uses in Safeguarding your data and its Privacy Policy.

Your rights

You may ask about personal data connected with this website, object to its use, or ask for correction or deletion where those rights apply. Please email a.maclullich@gmail.com or use my University of Edinburgh profile. You can also raise a concern with the Information Commissioner's Office.

Other websites

This website links to external sites, including university, research, professional and public resource websites. Those services have their own privacy practices. Following a link takes you to a separate website whose operator is responsible for it.
